Refund policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Short version: you pay nothing to use this website or send an enquiry, and we do not take payments on it. This policy explains what to expect if you later agree paid work with MIT.

1. Enquiries and website

Free. There is nothing to refund.

2. Paid project work

Before you pay, MIT will give you a written quote or agreement that states:

  • the total price, including taxes and any other charges, so there are no hidden fees;
  • what is included and what is not;
  • the payment schedule or milestones; and
  • the cancellation and refund terms for that project.

As a general principle, if you cancel or we cannot deliver, money paid for work that has not been started or delivered will be refunded to the payment method you used. How much has been “started” is set out in your agreement, so please read it before you pay.

3. Subscriptions and automatic renewals

MIT does not currently offer any. If we ever do, we will explain the price, the renewal date and how to cancel before you sign up, remind you before each renewal, and let you cancel in the same way you signed up.

4. How to ask for a refund or cancel

Email mi.technologiesprvt@gmail.com with the name on the agreement and what you would like to do. We will confirm in writing and tell you how long a refund will take. Your rights under consumer law are not affected by this policy.
