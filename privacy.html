Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026 (version 2026-11-17)

This policy explains what personal information MIT collects through this website, why, who else handles it, how long we keep it and how you can control it. We aim to follow India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025 as they come into force, and the Information Technology Act, 2000. We have written it in plain language on purpose.

2. What we collect, and why

We only collect what we need to answer your enquiry. There are no accounts, no payments and no tracking on this website.

We do not ask for passwords, payment details, government ID numbers or other sensitive information, and we ask you not to send any. We do not store your IP address, browser details or the page you were on with your enquiry. Our service providers (section 6) may process technical data such as your IP address simply to deliver the website and receive your form.

3. How we use it

  • To read and reply to your enquiry and discuss next steps. This is the only reason we need the details you type into the form.
  • To keep a record of your consent, and to handle your data requests and complaints.
  • To meet legal obligations, and to protect ourselves in a dispute.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use automated decision-making about you.

4. Marketing emails (optional, separate)

We send you marketing emails only if you tick the separate, unticked-by-default box on the form. Ticking it is never required to send an enquiry. Every marketing email will include a working unsubscribe link, and you can also unsubscribe at any time by emailing mi.technologiesprvt@gmail.com. We act on unsubscribe requests promptly, and we aim to do so within 7 days. After you unsubscribe we may still send messages about an enquiry you started or a project you have agreed with us.

5. AI and your information

We do not use your enquiry details to train AI models and we do not give them to anyone else for that purpose. Nobody has our permission to collect this website’s content to train AI models; our robots.txt asks AI crawlers to stay away.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

This website does not currently use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, pixels or SDKs. If we ever add any, they will stay switched off until you accept them in the cookie banner, and we will update this page first. You can change your choice at any time using the “Cookie settings” button in the footer or .

7. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not become a project: deleted 12 months after the date of the enquiry or our last message to you, whichever is later.
  • Projects we agree with you: kept for the life of the agreement, then only as long as the law or a possible dispute requires, then deleted.
  • Marketing consent: kept until you withdraw it, plus a short record that you withdrew so we do not email you again.
  • Data requests and complaints: kept for 3 years as proof of how we handled them.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including limiting who on our team can see enquiries and using the security features of the services in section 6. No system or internet transmission is completely secure. If a breach affecting you occurs, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Your rights and how to use them

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information of yours we hold and what we do with it;
  • correct or complete it;
  • delete it;
  • stop using it where you have withdrawn consent (withdrawing is as easy as giving it);
  • deal with a complaint; and
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights for you if you die or cannot act for yourself.

How: send us a data request by email from the address you used in the enquiry, or message us on the numbers in section 13. We may ask for a little information to confirm it is you, so we do not give your data to someone else. We aim to respond within 30 days. Deletion requests are free; we will delete your information from our enquiry records unless the law requires us to keep it, and we will tell you if so.

If we do not resolve your complaint, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India as the law provides.

10. Children

This website is meant for people aged 18 or over. The enquiry form asks you to confirm you are 18 or older, and asks anyone under 18 to have a parent or guardian send the enquiry. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 without the verifiable consent of a parent or lawful guardian. We do not track or profile children or show them targeted advertising. If we learn that we hold a child’s information without that consent, we will delete it.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and, where the change affects how we use information you already gave us, ask for your agreement again.
